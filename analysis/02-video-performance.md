## 3. Kya Chala vs Kya Dooba (Performance Patterns)

### ✅ JO CHALA (mega hits ka common DNA)

| Factor | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **Roz-marra ka relatable topic + hidden dark truth** | Grass→Milk (23M) — har ghar ka doodh, shocking process |
| **Gross/shock curiosity** | Vulture poop (2.9M), Pregnant Sharks (3.9M) — "eww/yikes" + "wow" combo |
| **India vs duniya pride angle** | UPI (3.4M), Weed (3.7M) — "America ko hairaan kar diya" |
| **Fear stakes (children/death)** | Polio (869K), Bihar fruit (201K), Rabies (320K) |
| **Hindi VO for mass topics** | Top 2 nature hits Hindi mein (23M, 2.9M) |
| **Title mein exact shocking image** | "Iron Coffins", "Poop on Its Own Feet", "Eating India's Rivers" |

### ❌ JO DOOBA (cold videos ka common DNA)

| Factor | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **Soft/abstract topics** | Duck sleep (9.1K), Cricket origin (7K), Camels myth (19K) |
| **Generic phrasing** | "Shocking History of the World's Deadliest Drug" (11K) — vague, no concrete image |
| **No India/relatable hook** | Pablo Escobar (10K) — Indian audience ko koi emotional connection nahi |
| **Low-stakes curiosity** | "Insect Chemical Weapon" (9.1K) — interesting but "so what?" |

### 📐 Title Formula (jo chala uska pattern)

```
[The/Why/How] + [Concrete Noun + Shock Modifier] + [Verb jo curiosity create kare] + [Emoji] + #facts #education

Examples:
✅ "The Disease That Trapped Children in Iron Coffins! 😨"   → concrete image + human stakes
✅ "Why Does This Vulture Poop on Its Own Feet? 🫪"          → gross question, must-know
✅ "THE WEED THAT INVADED INDIA"                            → CAPS + national stakes
❌ "Duck That Sleeps With One Eye Open"                      → no stakes, no emotion
```

**Emoji system:** 😨 = fear/shock, 🫪 = weird/disgusting wonder, 🥛🦒🦎 = topic icon (rare)
**Hashtags:** `#facts #education` mandatory; `#india` on India topics; `#cowmilk` type niche tags occasionally.

## 5. Is Catalog Se Skill Ke Liye Rules

1. **Topic filter (5-question test):** Kya is fact mein (a) shock image hai? (b) human/India stakes hain? (c) ek concrete noun hai title mein? (d) "sun ke kisi ko batane ka mann kare"? (e) 60s mein 3 escalating wow-moments possible hain? — 4/5 se kam = topic reject
2. **Batch production:** ek din 2-3 topics shortlist karo, viral tiers target karo (≥1 "HUGE-style" topic per week)
3. **Title pehle likho** (script se pehle) — agar title boring lage, topic hi badal do
4. **Hindi-first** for daily-life/nature mass topics; **English** for geopolitics/medical/global
5. Failures se daro mat — 8 delete hue phir bhi channel 42M pe hai; **volume + filter** hi strategy hai
