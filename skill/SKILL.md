# SKILL — AI Facts Video Editor (Creates by Gulshan Style + Better)

> **Yeh skill kya karti hai:** "Facts that feel unreal" style ke YouTube Shorts + Long-form videos banana — **exact Creates by Gulshan format** (script formula, editing pacing, sound design) — aur usse **behtar** execution ke saath.
>
> **Level:** Beginner → Advanced. Ek insaan + AI tools = pura studio.
> **Derived from:** 7 full transcript analyses, 38-video performance data, sound-placement evidence (analysis/ folder).

---

## When To Use

- Kisi bhi "shocking/unreal fact" ko viral short ya documentary video mein convert karna ho
- Science / nature / history / India-facts topic ho
- AI se script, voice, visuals aur edit banana ho (single-creator pipeline)
- Goal ho: **high retention, high share, cinematic feel**

## Inputs (Shuru Karne Se Pehle)

1. **Topic** (ya topic bucket: daily-science / nature-dark / India-pride / medical-history / disaster)
2. **Target:** Short (~2-3 min) ya Long-form (6-9 min)
3. **Language:** VO = **Hindi fixed** (see rule below), on-screen text = English fixed
4. **Visual engine:** Step 5 mein emotion ke hisaab se choose hoga (3 engines available)

## Output (Definition of Done)

- [x] Script (formula-compliant) — 330-380 words (short) / 1,100-1,250 words (long)
- [x] VO audio (AI voice, warm, fast-natural)
- [x] Edit (timeline templates se) — cut rate 2.5-3s, sound design 3-layer
- [x] Thumbnail (3 variants) + Title (5 variants) + Description + Hashtags
- [x] QC checklist pass (Step 10)

## STEP 0 — Topic Filter (5-Question Test)

Har topic par yeh 5 sawaal. **≥4 YES** hi aage badhega:

1. Kya title mein ek **concrete shock image** hai? (iron coffin / poop on feet / black gold)
2. Kya **stakes** hain — death, disease, money, nation, ya daily life ka hidden danger?
3. Kya **3-4 escalating wow-facts** milenge (numbers ke saath)?
4. Kya sun ke **kisi ko batane ka mann** karega (share trigger)?
5. Kya isme **emotional turn** possible hai (pride, awe, mother-love, genius)?

**Bonus rule (data se):** daily-life topics (doodh, paani, ghar ki cheez) + nature gross-mysteries = MEGA potential. Soft trivia (cricket history, chai history) = skip.

## STEP 1 — Title First (5 Variants)

Formula:
```
[The/Why/How] + [Concrete Noun + Shock Modifier] + [Curiosity Verb] + [Emoji] + #facts #education
```
- 3 fear/shock variants + 2 question variants
- Emoji: 😨 (fear) / 🫪 (gross-wonder) / topic icon sparingly
- Hindi variant for mass topics: "गाय घास से दूध नहीं बनाती... 😨 | The Shocking Truth"
- **Test:** title ko aadha padh ke chhod do — curiosity rukni chahiye. Nahi ruki → topic badlo.

## STEP 2 — Research (Fact Pack Banao)

- 8-12 verified facts + 5-6 concrete numbers (source links save karo)
- Ek **timeline/sequence** (origin → problem → solution → twist → future)
- 1-2 **quote/dialogue** (real person ho to — e.g., Salk's "Could you patent the sun?")
- **Fact-check pass:** har number ka 2nd source. (Original channel ke kuchh numbers loose hain — yeh tumhari advantage hai)

## STEP 3 — Script (Formula se Likho)

### ⛓️ 4 INVARIANT LAWS (frame-level teardown se confirmed — kabhi mat todo)

1. **Contrast Hook in First 10 Seconds** — do opposite cheezon ki takkar (*Kudrat vs Lab Monster | Khoonkhar Shark vs Maa ki Mamta | Richest USA vs ₹10 Golgappa*)
2. **Hindi Audio + English Visual Labels** — VO 100% Hindi (dramatic), on-screen text 100% English (2-3 word punch labels). Dual-coding = swipe-away rate girta hai
3. **Zero Static Frames (2-Second Rule)** — har 2 second mein ya counter chal raha hai, ya stamp gir raha hai, ya line/map move kar rahi hai
4. **Shorts mein CTA BILKUL NAHI** — "like/subscribe" mat bolo. Video ek powerful emotional punchline + visual loop par khatam karo (long-form mein pride-CTA allowed hai)

### SHORTS TEMPLATE (~350-400 words → ~2-3 min Hindi narration)

```
① HOOK (2-3 lines, ≤25 words):
   "Would you believe / क्या आपको लगता है..." + shock image + stakes promise

② STINGER (1-2 lines):
   "A [mind-blowing/dark/emotional] truth of science and [domain]
    that will [change your perspective / force you to think]..."

③ SETUP (4-5 lines):
   Context + relatable contrast ("For humans X... but for Y...")

④ ESCALATION (12-16 lines):
   3-4 facts, har ek pichle se bada. Numbers har 2-3 lines mein.
   Har fact ke baad ek micro-cliffhanger ("But then...")

⑤ TWIST (2-3 lines):
   "But here's the real truth..." / "लेकिन असली सच अब शुरू होता है..."
   + silence-worthy reveal

⑥ EMOTIONAL TURN (3-4 lines):
   Reframe: awe / moral / mother-love / genius-of-nature

⑦ POETIC CLOSE (1-2 lines):
   Quotable line. Last word = loop-echo of first word/image.
```

### LONG-FORM TEMPLATE (~1,150 words / 8 min)

```
① CINEMATIC COLD OPEN (40-50s): "Imagine..." scene painting
② IDENTITY REVEAL: "This is not a movie. This is [TOPIC]."
③ ORIGIN STORY (2-2.5 min): dated crisis + emotional national/human stakes
④ PROBLEM (1 min): "But there was a massive problem..."
⑤ SOLUTION + SCIENCE (1.5-2 min): engineering reveal + "Now, understand this carefully"
⑥ TWIST CHAPTER (50s): "But the story doesn't end here" — smart-play example
⑦ SCALE + FUTURE (40s): numbers ("How much is that?") + expansion
⑧ CALLBACK + PRIDE CTA (15-20s): ending line = opening ka mirror + like/share/subscribe
```

### Writing Rules (Non-negotiable)
1. Sentences 8-14 words; punch lines 3-5 words
2. Har 12-18 words par idea/scene change
3. Direct address: "Think about it", "जरा सोचिए", "Now understand this carefully"
4. Rhetorical Q bridges: "But the question is...?", "How much is that?"
5. Ek **dialogue reenactment** (agar real character ho)
6. Ek **personification** (villain/hero/genius character banao topic ka)
7. Read-aloud test — bol ke sunao, saans ruk wahan punctuation fix
8. **Language decision:** daily-life/nature mass topic → **Hindi**; geopolitics/medical/global → **English** (ya Hindi-first with EN captions)

## STEP 4 — Voice Over (AI VO + Micro-Acting)

- Tool: **ElevenLabs** (Multilingual v2/v3) ya Murf — **Hindi voice, warm dramatic narrator** (dost chai pe kahani suna raha hai, news anchor nahi)
- Voice pick: male 30s-40s, credible + emotional range. Robotic TTS mat use karo
- Settings: stability 45-55%, clarity 70-80%, speed 1.0-1.08x (Hindi mein natural pauses chhodo)
- **Micro-Acting (confirmed techniques):**
  - Chhota **chuckle/smirk** taunt lines ke baad ("...toh woh ek sir-dard hai... *hehe*")
  - **Taunting/sarcastic tone** pride moments par (Google letter quote jaisi lines)
  - **0.5s micro-pause + pitch raise** reveal se pehle ("...lekin Thai Mangur? *pause*")
  - **Whispery, dheemi aawaz** raat/mystery/andhera scenes par
  - **Voice softening** emotional climax par (1:39 jaisa — naram, bhaavuk)
- **Delivery arc:** hook = conspiratorial intensity → escalation = building urgency → twist = slow 10% + gravity → emotion = soft warm → punchline = pride + slight smile
- Output: WAV 48kHz, 3 takes per section, best lo

## STEP 5 — Visual Engine Selection + Assembly

### 🎨 3 VISUAL ENGINES (emotion ke hisaab se choose karo — "Gulshan Formula")

| Engine | Kab use karo | Style |
|---|---|---|
| **A. 2D Retro Comic / Halftone (Risograph)** | Disgust, horror, dark/gory topics (deadly fish, disease) — **YouTube policy hack:** asli graphic footage se strike/disgust bachata hai | Dot-texture comic frames, bold comic headers, pink stamps, 2D recurring characters (farmer/scientist/mafia/consumer) |
| **B. 3D Hyper-Real AI + Face-Cam + HUD** | Awe, emotion, nature, mother-kind stories (sharks, animals) | Realistic AI clips + **face-cam trust hook** (0-8s split-screen, 1:06 par 2s reset) + sci-fi HUD callouts (target dot ⦿ + line + tiny English label) + X-ray cutaways |
| **C. Dark-Mode Vector Motion Infographic** | Systems, economics, tech, geopolitics (UPI, policy) | Neon vector icons, data animations, maps, code terminals, counters, stamps — zero stock footage |

### Universal UI Retention Frame (Engine C par, optional others)
- **Top orange progress bar** (0→100% video ke saath) = completion bias
- **🔴 red recording dot + slanted ribbon + ruler ticks (||||)** = "classified dossier" look

### Assembly Rules
Script ke har clause ke liye shot list banao (text-to-visual literalism):

| Script element | Visual |
|---|---|
| Concrete noun ("iron coffin") | Us cheez ka exact shot (engine ke style mein) |
| Number ("500 liters") | Animated counter / stat card (meter bhaagta hua) |
| Strong word (BANNED, SMUGGLED) | **Stamp THUD** (pink/red, screen par girta hai) |
| History ("1990, August") | Calendar flip / archival look + date |
| Map/place | Animated map + pin/dots explosion + travelling lines |
| Science mechanism | X-ray cutaway / animated diagram + HUD labels |
| Contrast (USA vs India) | Split-screen (opposites collide) |
| Emotion (mother-love) | Slow cinematic close-up + warm grade |

Sources: **Midjourney/Runway (AI)** + stock (sparingly) + After Effects/CapCut motion graphics.
**Consistency lock:** ek video mein EK engine — style mix mat karo (alag videos mein alag engine allowed).

## STEP 6 — Edit (Timeline Templates se + Motion Engine)

Full timeline specs: `templates/editing-timelines.md`. **Production engine: `motion-engine.md` (zaroor padho — static slideshow = instant reject).** Core rules:

1. **Zero Static Frames:** koi frame 1s+ static nahi. Camera hamesha zinda (push/pull + wobble + breathing pulse + film-weave jitter); print-boil halftone texture
2. **Cut rate:** shorts 2-2.5s/shots; long-form 3-5s. Har cut = **whip-pan/slide transition** + flash + whoosh (hard cut sirf deliberate impact par)
3. **Face-cam trust hook:** pehle 8s split-screen (upar engine visual, neeche tum mic ke saath) → 0:09 se full-bleed. **Twist par 2s face-cam reset** (visual fatigue todta hai)
4. **HUD callouts** (engine B): target dot ⦿ → growing line → tiny English label (bade noisy captions nahi)
5. **Stamps & counters:** strong words par SLAM stamp (overshoot drop + shake + starbursts); numbers par **ROLLING counters** jo noisy intermediates se guzar ke snap karein (money-meter feel)
6. **Kinetic type:** labels word-by-word pop-in (ease-out-back overshoot) — static text kabhi nahi
7. **Pattern interrupt** har 8-12s (visual grammar change: punch-in close-up → wide → text beat → HUD)
8. **Twist moment:** 0.8s music hole → glitch/boom impact frame
9. **Visual loop ending:** last frame = first frame ka echo (whip-in). Shorts ka end = emotional punchline, **CTA nahi**

## STEP 7 — Sound Design (3-Layer + Confirmed Micro-SFX Map)

```
Layer 1: MUSIC BED     — engine ke hisaab se: Comic→thriller/horror | 3D→emotional piano+drone | Vector→driving synth-pulse
Layer 2: ACCENT SFX    — micro-SFX har ~3s (neeche map), stamps, impacts, risers
Layer 3: TEXTURE       — ambience (underwater muffle, wind, crowd), heartbeat rumble, grain
```

### Confirmed Micro-SFX Map (har ~3 second ear-candy):

| Trigger | SFX |
|---|---|
| Animal/action | water splash |
| Lab/science | electric zap + coin clink |
| Strong word (BANNED, SMUGGLED) | heavy stamp thud |
| Money/counter | cash register tick |
| Weather/disaster | thunder + rain |
| Reveal/tear | paper tear |
| HUD callout pop | futuristic UI click/whoosh |
| Womb/depth/close scenes | underwater muffle + heartbeat rumble |
| Map/line motion | whoosh + digital tick |
| Twist | riser → silence 0.2s → BOOM |
| Emotional turn | ambient piano swell |
| End punchline | resolve chord + fade |

Mix: VO -14 LUFS integrated, music -20 LUFS ducked, SFX transients -8 to -12. Phone-speaker test zaroor.

## STEP 8 — Captions & Text

- Auto-captions → **styled keyword captions** (Anton/Bebas + Montserrat)
- Keyword highlights: yellow/red; numbers always highlighted
- Hindi: Devanagari captions (readability high)
- Position: middle-lower third (Shorts UI safe zone)

## STEP 9 — Packaging (Title, Thumbnail, Description)

- **Thumbnail (long-form):** 3 variants — (a) object + big number, (b) split before/after, (c) face/emotion + text. Text ≤5 words. Mobile 20% size test.
- **Title A/B:** curiosity gap, no spoiler, emoji 1 max
- **Description:** 2-3 line hook + timestamps (long-form) + sources + "Made with AI" note + hashtags `#facts #education #india`
- **Shorts:** pinned comment with question (engagement bait), 2nd comment with "Part 2?" loop

## STEP 10 — QC Gate (Publish se Pehle — ab TOOL-BACKED)

**Mandatory tool checks (numbers, opinions nahi):**

```bash
# PASS: avg motion > 5.0 | longest static run < 1.0s | static frames < 5%

```

## STEP 11 — Publish Rhythm & Feedback Loop

- **Cadence:** 1 short/day (min), 2/day during growth push; long-form 1/week (jab tak CTR fix na ho)
- **Best time (India audience):** 12-2 PM ya 7-10 PM IST
- **Feedback loop:** 48h mein CTR + AVD check. CTR < 6% → title/thumbnail badlo. AVD < 60% (short) → hook weak.
- **Scaling:** jo topic 500K+ jaye → us bucket ke 3 aur variations banao (series without branding)

## Anti-Patterns (Kabhi Mat Karo)

1. ❌ **Static photo + caption slideshow** — client-tested REJECT ("1% bhi nahi"). Motion engine mandatory
2. ❌ VO ko chunks mein todna / beech mein gaps daalna — client law: voice continuous
3. ❌ Text jo clip ho ya ek baar aake baithe — kinetic type + auto-fit mandatory
4. ❌ Counters jo seedha final value dikhayein — real numbers **bhaagte hain** phir snap
5. ❌ Hard cuts bina transition — whoosh/whip/flash lagao
6. ❌ Abstract titles ("Amazing Facts About Nature") — curiosity gap zero
7. ❌ Soft trivia topics bina stakes ke (data: 7-12K views cap)
8. ❌ Music VO se loud — clarity first
9. ❌ Facts bina source ke (AI hallucination risk) — trust hi brand hai
10. ❌ 60s mein 10 facts daalna — 3-4 escalating facts better hain
